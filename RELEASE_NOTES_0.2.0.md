# VersoFold 0.2.0

September 23, 2026 · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

VersoFold 0.2.0 brings the paper, its evidence, data, figures, and review workflow into one local workspace.

## Find your way around

- Four shorter menus organize the toolset: **Write**, **Research**, **Review**, and **Publish**. Search any tool, action, or project file with **⌘K**.
- A searchable, offline Markdown handbook covers writing, reading, research, advanced workflows, and LaTeX fundamentals. Open it from Home, Help, or the editor and reader you are using.
- **Explore workflows** offers practical paths through the tools. **Try a practice project** creates a separate manuscript with synthetic data, an editable figure and table, a linked result, a manuscript board, and a hands-on walkthrough.
- Saved workspace arrangements and responsive layouts help keep the document readable as panels open and windows resize.
- Draft recovery now also runs when background metadata has already loaded a document, preserving unsaved work when reopening the editor.

## From data to manuscript

- **Figure Studio** adds richer data preparation, chart types, uncertainty displays, multi-panel layouts, editable styling, reusable paper themes, generated tables, and publication exports. Linked datasets and recorded recipes keep generated assets connected to their inputs.
- The **Table editor** offers spreadsheet editing, formulas, formatting, and a publication preview. Save an editable sheet, publish its LaTeX, and insert it into the manuscript.
- **Linked results** connects manuscript values and statements to data. Changed inputs can suggest updated numbers and flag associated claims for author review.
- The **Manuscript board** organizes sections, writing goals, notes, and checklists. The **Page layout** workbench inspects page balance and figure and table placement.

## Read, review, and submit

- The **Reading desk** organizes a reading queue, compares papers side by side, and saves passages and notes with page links and Markdown export.
- **Evidence Lens** searches attached sources and helps trace manuscript claims to supporting passages. Saved evidence and research records keep sources and author decisions visible.
- **Submission rehearsal** records venue requirements and checks a compiled paper against them. The **Rebuttal** workspace connects reviewer feedback, planned revisions, responses, and supporting evidence.
- Create a **Web edition** with its own public-facing text, design, figures, and export. Expanded arXiv packaging complements Word, Overleaf, and Git workflows.

## Work with your preferred agent

- Run connected **Codex**, **Claude Code**, and compatible **ACP** conversations inside VersoFold, with model and thinking controls, writing styles, approval choices, usage information, saved conversations, and stop controls.
- **External agents** has one settings page for connection setup, installed runtimes, project briefs, and completion reports. Open it from the Workspace gear or a connected conversation’s **Agent settings**. Per-conversation agent selection and access grants live in **Conversation options**.
- Scoped external connections let supported desktop and remote clients use VersoFold’s project tools. Research, evidence, figures, tables, boards, and submission tools are also available to the built-in agent.
- Agents can search and read the handbook on request, or when stuck on a specific VersoFold capability. Documentation is not automatically added to every conversation.
- **Support VersoFold** remains in Help and is now available in both the settings window and gear menu.
