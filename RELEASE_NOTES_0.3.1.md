# VersoFold 0.3.1

September 30, 2026 · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

A small update to [VersoFold 0.3.0](https://github.com/vicliv/versofold-releases/releases/tag/v0.3.0), which brought live co-editing, track changes, automatic two-way Overleaf sync and agent notifications.

- **Drag papers into your library.** Drag a paper from your browser, either the tab's address or a link on any publisher, arXiv or DOI page, onto the Reference library or the editor's **Add a paper** dialog. It is saved straight away and its metadata is fetched.
- **Clearer public links.** The share panel now explains that a public link is a Cloudflare tunnel from your computer. It works only while the computer is awake and VersoFold is running; closing the lid, sleeping, shutting down or quitting closes it, and reopening gives a new address.
