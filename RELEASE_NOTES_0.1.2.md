# VersoFold 0.1.2

September 20, 2026 · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Choose the DMG for your Mac below, then drag VersoFold to Applications. Both builds are signed and Apple-notarized.

## What’s new

- Open a PDF directly from Home, Finder, or **Open With → VersoFold**. VersoFold keeps a reading copy, extracts page text, links citations to bibliography entries, and gives the agent text-first access with page-image inspection when needed.
- Reader references now use compact title, author, year, and venue cards in first-citation order. Crossref matching distinguishes strong evidence from uncertain candidates, with direct paper search, PDF download, and library actions.
- PDF links navigate to internal destinations or external pages. Citation clicks now coordinate highlighting across LaTeX, Visual, PDF, and References views, while PDF comment quotes retain the exact selected text.
- **Follow agent edits** marks removals in red and additions in green. Accept or reject individual changes inline, approve a whole file, or review project-wide changes with stale-comparison protection and recoverable baselines.
- The agent composer includes a searchable prompt library for reading, reviewing, reproducibility, citation checking, and writing. Model effort and authorization choices persist across restarts.
- Highlight text in an agent conversation to show **Add to chat** directly above the selection, keeping the excerpt close to the passage you are reusing.
- Home and Settings now include **Request a feature** alongside private bug reporting.

PDF extraction and citation matching are heuristic. Scanned PDFs still require page-image inspection; OCR is not included in this release. Metadata lookup requires a network connection, and publisher downloads may require access through the publisher’s website.
